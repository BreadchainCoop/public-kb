# Governance

Bread Cooperative is a worker-owned organisation. There is no CEO and no outside
shareholders. The people who do the work are the people who make the decisions.

## How we make decisions

Decisions are made collectively rather than by any single person. Everyday work
is coordinated in weekly standups and an open community call, while larger
choices about strategy, budgets, membership, and the direction of our tools are
deliberated and made together. Our aim is simple: the people affected by a
decision should have a say in it.

Our principles and processes are documented publicly in the docs:

- [Bread Constitution](https://docs.bread.coop/organization/bread-constitution/) — shared purpose and guiding principles
- [Operational Annex](https://docs.bread.coop/organization/operational-annex/) — membership, responsibilities, and financial governance

## Yield governance

Every month, the yield generated by the Solidarity Fund is distributed to member
projects through a community vote — participatory budgeting on-chain. Half of
each distribution is split evenly across all member projects; the other half is
allocated by a token vote, where voting power reflects the average BREAD you held
over the previous month.
