# Solidarity Tools

## Solidarity Fund

Give without giving. Deposit xDAI into the Solidarity Fund and mint BREAD at a
1:1 ratio. Your deposit generates yield that is distributed monthly to member
projects by community vote. Your principal is always redeemable — burn BREAD
back to xDAI at any time, 1:1, with no penalty or waiting period.

App: [fund.bread.coop](https://fund.bread.coop)

## Stacks

Savings circles for those closest to you. Members pool BREAD at regular
intervals and take turns receiving the full pot — the centuries-old rotating
savings model, now running on tamper-proof escrow, with no bank, no lender, and
no single person holding the funds.

App: [stacks.bread.coop](https://stacks.bread.coop)
