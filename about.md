# About Bread Cooperative

Bread Cooperative is a worker-owned collective building financial tools for
cooperatives, NGOs, and solidarity-focused communities. Everything we build is
open source. Every decision is made collectively — there is no CEO and no
outside shareholders.

## BREAD, our community currency

BREAD is the cooperative's community currency, running on Gnosis Chain. It is
stable and spendable, and every token in circulation is backed 1:1 by xDAI, a
stablecoin pegged to the US Dollar.

What makes BREAD different from other stablecoins is what happens to the
interest. When you mint BREAD through the Solidarity Fund, your xDAI is put to
work generating yield. That yield funds the cooperative and its member projects,
distributed every month by a community vote. Your principal stays intact — only
the earnings are shared.

Learn more in the [docs](https://docs.bread.coop).
